Homelab Agent

Homelab Agent is a private, personal tool. It is used by exactly one person, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else.

It lets an AI assistant running on the owner's own home server read and manage the owner's own Google account — Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Tasks, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides and Google Meet — when the owner asks it to, or as part of automations the owner has set up.

There is no sign-up, no public service, and no other users.
