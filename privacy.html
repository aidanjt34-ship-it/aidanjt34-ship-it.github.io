Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

Who this applies to

Homelab Agent is a private tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.

What Google data it can access

With the owner's consent, it requests access to: Gmail (read, compose, send, organise and move to trash), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts and "other contacts" (read), the directory (read), Google Tasks, Google Docs, Google Sheets, Google Slides, Google Meet spaces, and the account's email address.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out what the owner asks for — for example summarising mail, managing calendar events or organising files — directly or through automations the owner has configured. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to build profiles.

To do this, content the assistant reads is processed by the AI model service that powers the assistant, only as needed to perform the owner's request.

Storage and sharing

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own home server, in files readable only by the system administrator account. Google user data is not shared with, or transferred to, any other party except the AI model service described above, and is not stored beyond what a requested task needs.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Homelab Agent's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored tokens are then deleted from the server.
